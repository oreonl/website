Deck of Many NPCs

Create, generate and organize every NPC in your D&D campaign. Every great campaign runs on great NPCs, and this puts a whole deck of them in your pocket.

  • Free
  • Built on the SRD 5.2.1 rules
  • Prebuilt deck included
  • Android

Everyone your party will ever meet

Start playing immediately with the included deck of prebuilt NPCs, or build your own. Hand-pick every detail, or let the app conjure a complete character with a single tap. Then keep your whole cast organized and level them up right alongside your party.

⚔️ Create any NPC

  • Prebuilt NPC deck included, ready to run at your table tonight
  • Full manual control over every choice, from species to alignment
  • Or randomly generate a complete NPC in seconds
  • Built on the latest 5e rules (SRD 5.2.1)

🎨 Bring them to life

  • Generate portraits for your characters
  • Generate bios based on an NPC's class, species, background, personality and alignment

🃏 Run your campaign

  • Organize NPCs into decks, by campaign, region, faction, or however you run your world
  • Track your NPCs between sessions and level them up as the story grows
  • Run fights with the built-in encounter tracker

🧪 Go homebrew

  • Create your own custom homebrew content
  • Import homebrew from other sources

Never be caught without the perfect NPC

Whether you're a first-time Dungeon Master prepping session one or a veteran GM improvising a tavern full of strangers.
