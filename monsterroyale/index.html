Monster Royale

Pick the monsters. Pick the battlefield. Then watch them fight it out, every roll played by the rules, or take command and play every turn yourself.

  • Free, no ads, no in-app purchases
  • Works offline, no account
  • 330 SRD 5.2.1 monsters
  • Android

Coming soon to Google Play

Monster battles, by the book

A rules engine plays every turn straight from the monsters' stat blocks: initiative, attack rolls against AC, saving throws, Advantage, cover, conditions, spells, breath weapons and legendary actions.

330 monsters

  • Every monster in the SRD 5.2.1, from goblins and wolves to giants, liches and ancient dragons
  • A full stat block for each, a tap away
  • Each one drawn in original isometric pixel art, with its own attacks and spells animated

Eight arenas that change the fight

  • Forest, swamp, lake shore, frozen lake, volcanic rift, ruins at night, an underdark cavern and open plains
  • Cover, high ground, difficult terrain, darkness, fog, deep water and slippery ice, all with real rules effects
  • Small to large battlefields

Watch, or take command

  • Auto-battle: build two teams or a free-for-all and watch it play out, with live win odds
  • Command mode: choose every move, attack, spell and target for your side
  • Aim breath weapons and spells, fly over the fight, and see your odds before you commit

Challenges

  • 15 hand-made battles that get harder as you go, from a goblin ambush to an adult red dragon
  • The same start every attempt, with fresh dice each time
  • Earn stars for winning fast and for each challenge's own feat

See who won, and why

  • A combat log of every roll, the MVP, and the battle's highlights
  • Watch any battle again, turn by turn
