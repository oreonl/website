First Roll

The 5.5e companion built for people who've never played a tabletop RPG before. Every term is explained. Every rule is at your fingertips. No prior experience required.

  • Free, no ads, no in-app purchases
  • Works offline, no account
  • All 12 SRD classes
  • Android

Your first session, handled

Whether you're rolling your first character or sitting down to your first session, First Roll handles the bookkeeping so you can focus on playing.

Built for beginners

  • Guided character builder with plain-English explanations
  • Tap any unfamiliar term to see what it means
  • Designed for true first-timers, no jargon assumed
  • Big, readable numbers and dark mode for the table

Full 5.5e character sheet

  • All 12 SRD classes and every SRD subclass, with level-gated reminders
  • Ability scores, skills, AC, initiative, speed
  • Inventory and equipment with magic item AC bonuses
  • Spells with prepared and known tracking
  • Backgrounds, feats, species traits, languages

Live session tracking

  • Hit points and temp HP with one-tap damage and healing
  • Spell slots by level: tap to spend, restore on long rest
  • Class resources: Rage, Focus Points, Bardic Inspiration, Channel Divinity, Sorcery Points, Wild Shape, Lay on Hands, Sneak Attack and more
  • Attack flow with advantage, Smite, Hex, Hunter's Mark, Bless and Wild Magic Surge triggers
  • Concentration, short and long rests, death saves, exhaustion and conditions

AI roleplay helper (optional)

  • Stuck on what your character would say or do? Get suggestions
  • Bring your own Anthropic Claude API key, or run a small model entirely on your device with no internet
  • Completely optional: the app works fully without AI

Designed for the table

  • Works offline, with no account, no signup and no ads
  • Six themes including high-contrast dark and bright daylight modes
  • OCR import: snap a photo of an existing paper character sheet
  • Built-in SRD reference: spells, conditions, rules, items, monsters
